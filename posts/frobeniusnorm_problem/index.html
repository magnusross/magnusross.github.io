Relationship between the Frobenius norm and singular values in SVD

2023/03/22

Problem

Note: I was brushing up on my SVD using the brilliant “Mathematics for Machine Learning” book, but the exercises listed in the book for SVD were a bit basic, so I decided to try use ChatGPT to generate a question. The problem below is what came out, quite impressive, although there were quite a few errors in the question that I had to fix (e.g the dimensionalities of the matrices).

Let A A A be an m×n m \times n m×n with m≥n m\geq n m≥n matrix with rank r r r, and let its singular value decomposition (SVD) be given by A=UΣVT A = U \Sigma V^T A=UΣVT, where U U U is an m×m m \times m m×m orthonormal matrix, Σ \Sigma Σ is an m×n m \times n m×n diagonal matrix with non-negative entries σ1≥σ2≥⋯≥σn>0 \sigma_1 \geq \sigma_2 \geq \dots \geq \sigma_n > 0 σ1​≥σ2​≥⋯≥σn​>0, and V V V is an n×n n \times n n×n orthonormal matrix. Show that the Frobenius norm of A A A is equal to the square root of the sum of the squares of the singular values.

Source: ChatGPT, Mathematics for Machine Learning by Deisenroth, Faisal and Ong

Solution

The Frobenius norm is given by,

∣∣A∣∣F2=∑i,jAi,j2 ||A||^2_F = \sum_{i,j}A^2_{i,j} ∣∣A∣∣F2​=i,j∑​Ai,j2​

which we can equivalently write (it is not so hard to show this) as

∣∣A∣∣F2=tr⁡(A⊤A). ||A||^2_F = \operatorname{tr}(A^\top A). ∣∣A∣∣F2​=tr(A⊤A).

Subbing in the SVD representation of A A A, we get

tr⁡(A⊤A)=tr⁡((UΣV⊤)⊤(UΣV⊤))=tr⁡(VΣ⊤U⊤UΣV⊤)=tr⁡(VΣ⊤ΣV⊤). \begin{align} \operatorname{tr}(A^\top A) &= \operatorname{tr}\left( (U\Sigma V^\top)^\top(U\Sigma V^\top)\right)\\ &= \operatorname{tr}\left(V\Sigma^\top U^\top U\Sigma V^\top\right) \\ &= \operatorname{tr}\left(V\Sigma^\top \Sigma V^\top\right). \end{align} tr(A⊤A)​=tr((UΣV⊤)⊤(UΣV⊤))=tr(VΣ⊤U⊤UΣV⊤)=tr(VΣ⊤ΣV⊤).​​

We can see by inspection that Σ⊤Σ=diag⁡(σ12,…,σn2)=Λ \Sigma^\top\Sigma = \operatorname{diag}(\sigma^2_1, \dots, \sigma^2_n)=\Lambda Σ⊤Σ=diag(σ12​,…,σn2​)=Λ. This implies that

[VΛV⊤]ii=σi2vi⊤vi=σi2, [V\Lambda V^\top]_{ii} = \sigma_i^2\mathbf{v}^\top_i\mathbf{v}_i = \sigma_i^2, [VΛV⊤]ii​=σi2​vi⊤​vi​=σi2​,

where vi \mathbf{v}_i vi​ is column i i i of V V V and the second equality follows from the othonormality of V V V. Putting it all together we get that

∣∣A∣∣F2=tr⁡(A⊤A)=∑iσi2, ||A||^2_F = \operatorname{tr}(A^\top A) = \sum_i \sigma_i^2, ∣∣A∣∣F2​=tr(A⊤A)=i∑​σi2​,

as required.
